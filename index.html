How it works

Three moves, no refills.

  1. Roll back and forth

    Use short strokes in both directions. The brush fabric grabs hair on the way out and the comb inside pulls it off on the way back.

  2. Press the release

    The chamber clicks open along the top so you can see what you picked up.

  3. Empty and go

    Tip the fur into the bin, close the lid, and it's ready for next time.

Where to use it

Made for fabric with a nap.

The brush needs a little texture to grip. It's at its best on upholstery and bedding.

  • Sofas & armchairs
  • Duvets & blankets
  • Car seats
  • Dog & cat beds
  • Wool coats
  • Carpet & rugs (short pile)
  • Fabric headboards
  • Not for skin or fur on your pet
  • Not for hard floors

FAQ

Questions.

Does it work on long-haired pets?

Yes. Long hair and short hair both collect in the chamber. Very fine undercoat may take a few more passes.

Can I use it directly on my dog or cat?

No. It's for fabric only. The brush isn't designed for skin or coats.

When will my order arrive?

Orders leave our warehouse within and arrive in . You'll get a tracking number by email when it ships.

What if it doesn't work for me?

You have 30 days from delivery to return it for a full refund of the item price. See our returns policy.

Does it work on cat hair?

Yes. Cat hair is finer than most dog hair, so use short, quick strokes in both directions and it collects in the chamber the same way.

How do I keep it working well?

Empty the chamber after each use so it doesn't overfill, and brush any lint off the fabric drum with your hand or a dry cloth. Don't wash it with water.
